Privacy policy

Effective 29 October 2026

AI Deck (the website at ai-deck.poollog-server.workers.dev and any domain it moves to) is run by Gabriel Ilustrisimo ("we"). It is a directory: it helps you find AI tools and shows how to install them. It is built to collect as little about you as possible. This policy says exactly what it does collect.

The short version

  • There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no ads.
  • Your searches run inside your browser and are not sent to us.
  • What you save stays on your own device.
  • If you submit a tool, we keep the link and the note you wrote, not who you are.

What stays on your device

The site stores a few settings in your browser's local storage so they survive a reload: the items you bookmark, your recent searches, the AI you picked in the "For" menu, and whether you chose New or Expert. This data never leaves your device. You can clear it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser, or by using "Clear" next to your recent searches.

If you arrive from ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, the page reads the address you came from (the referrer) in your browser to pre-select that AI. It is not sent anywhere.

What reaches our server

Tool submissions. When you use "Submit a tool", we store the link you entered, the kind you picked, the optional note, and the time. We do not store your IP address or any identifier with it. Submissions are kept until they are reviewed and either added to the catalog or deleted. Do not put personal information in the note.

AI ranking. The site can ask an AI model to rank search results. This is currently switched off. If it is switched on, your search words and the list of matching tools are sent to the AI provider we use (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or OpenRouter) to produce the ranking; nothing that identifies you is included. That provider's own privacy policy then applies to that request. This policy will be updated before that changes.

Rate limiting. To stop abuse, requests to the site's API are counted per IP address for about a minute. The count is not stored by us.

Services that see your connection

Cloudflare hosts the site. Like any web host it receives your IP address, browser details and the pages you request, and it keeps request logs for a few days so problems can be found. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Google Fonts serves the site's typefaces and icons, so your browser requests them from Google, which receives your IP address and browser details. See Google's privacy policy.

Links you follow. Listings link to GitHub, to the tools' own sites, and to ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. Once you open one, that site's privacy policy applies. Tools you install from a listing are run by their own publishers, not by us.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share personal information, build profiles, track you across sites, or use your data to train AI models.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is normally nothing to access, correct or delete. If you think a submission contains information about you, or you have any other privacy question or request, open an issue on the project's GitHub page and we will deal with it. Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, the UK or California) you may have further rights under local law, and you can complain to your data protection authority.

Children

AI Deck is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date. The history of every change is public in the project's source code on GitHub.
